title: AprilTag Support
description: Use known AprilTags to anchor Mighty poses, correct drift, and align multiple cameras in a shared coordinate frame.
---

Mighty can use AprilTags as fixed landmarks for visual-inertial odometry (VIO).
Give each tag a physical size, mounting orientation, and known position. When
the camera sees a matching tag, it corrects its reported position and orientation
to match your chosen coordinates.
VIO continues tracking between sightings.

Once configured, AprilTag support stays active while VIO is running. You can
close the Visualizer and the camera will continue using your saved tags.

## What you can use it for

- **A repeatable origin:** put a tag at a fixed reference point, such as a
  workbench or docking station. Looking at it aligns each new VIO session to
  the same coordinates.
- **Drift correction at checkpoints:** place known tags along a route so the
  camera can realign its output when it revisits those locations.
- **Shared coordinates across cameras:** configure the same tag map on multiple
  Mighty cameras, then let each camera observe a configured tag. Their reported
  trajectories can be displayed in the same world frame.
- **Repeatable experiments and demos:** use a fixed tag to compare trajectories
  across runs instead of relying on where each camera happened to initialize.

## Prepare a tag

Use a **tag36h11** AprilTag with a one-cell black border. Mount it flat and keep
it stationary.

Measure one side of the tag's outer black square, excluding the surrounding
white margin. Enter that measurement in **centimeters**. For example, a black
square measuring 16.2 cm across needs a size of `16.2`, regardless of the paper
size. Keep the white margin visible around the tag.

The camera needs a valid saved camera/IMU calibration. Follow
[Calibration](/docs/calibration) if you have not calibrated it, or if the lens or
mount has changed.

## Configure tags in the Visualizer

1. Connect the camera and open the [Visualizer](/viz). Wait for it to connect.
2. If VIO is running, click **Stop VIO**.
3. Open **Device Settings** and find **Relocalization**.
4. Click **Relocalize using AprilTags**, or **Edit Tags** to change existing rows.
5. Fill in the tag's ID, size, orientation, and position using the table below.
   Use **Add Row** for additional tags and **-** to remove a row.
6. Click **Save**. The button in Device Settings will show **AprilTags On**.
7. Click **Start VIO**, allow tracking to initialize, and point the camera at a
   configured tag.

*The Visualizer's configuration modal, rendered with example values.*

| Field | What to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Tag ID** | The printed tag's numeric ID, such as `7`, or `*` to match any ID. An explicit ID takes precedence over a wildcard. |
| **Size cm** | Side length of the outer black square in centimeters. The default is `16.2`; replace it with your measurement. |
| **Orientation** | **Upright** for a vertical tag, or **Facing up** for a horizontal tag with its printed face pointing upward. |
| **X, Y, Z** | Position of the **tag center** in your world frame, in **meters**. These are not the camera's position or the distance from the camera to the tag. |

For a first test, use one upright tag with its actual ID and size, and set
`X = 0`, `Y = 0`, `Z = 0`. This makes the tag center the origin. The camera's
reported position will be offset from that origin according to where it is
relative to the tag.

### Orientation and multiple tags

Rotating the printed tag changes the camera's reported orientation. Choose
**Upright** or **Facing up** to match how it is mounted. Custom mounting angles
are not supported. An upright tag faces **+X** in your chosen coordinates; a
facing-up tag faces **+Z**.

When adding multiple landmarks, measure all tag centers in one common frame
and mount each tag consistently with its preset. Tags on walls facing different
directions cannot be described just by choosing **Upright** and changing XYZ.
Use unique IDs for tags at different positions.

The `*` wildcard is convenient for a single-tag trial, but it assigns the same
size, orientation, and position to every otherwise unmatched tag. It does not
automatically discover a map of tags. Use explicit IDs for a room with multiple
landmarks.

### Saving, editing, and disabling

**Save** stores the settings on the connected camera. They survive a reboot,
and each camera has its own settings.

**Stop and restart VIO to apply changes.** The **AprilTags On** label confirms
that your settings are saved. Look for the relocalization notification to confirm
that the camera has used a tag.

To disable, stop VIO, then click **Disable** in the modal or click **AprilTags On**
in Device Settings. Both clear the saved tag configuration. Start VIO again to
track without tag relocalization. Copy down your rows first if you want to reuse
them; disabling is not a pause that preserves the rows. **Close** dismisses the
modal without saving draft edits.

## Check frequency

Mighty automatically checks for configured tags about **every 2 seconds** while
VIO is running and continues tracking between checks. You do not need to trigger
each check. The check frequency cannot be changed in the Visualizer.

## Check that it is working

Keep a configured tag fully visible and hold the camera steadily for a few
seconds; a brief glimpse may be missed.
The Visualizer can show detected tag outlines, and a successful alignment
produces a notification such as **Tag #7 detected, relocalizing**.

The displayed position and orientation may jump as a tag corrects accumulated
drift. Tracking continues afterward, and drift can build up again between tag
sightings.

If you see no alignment, check that VIO has initialized, the calibration is
installed, the tag is from the correct family, and its ID matches a saved row.
If the position or orientation is wrong, check the measured size, centimeter
versus meter units, tag-center coordinates, and mounting rotation. Move closer
or improve lighting if the corners are small, blurred, obscured, or affected by
glare. A detection overlay alone does not confirm a successful pose alignment.

## Use multiple cameras in one frame

First follow [Multi-Camera Setup](/docs/multi-camera) to give each camera a
different USB IP range. Save the same tag map on each camera, then start VIO on
each and let it see a configured tag. Each camera aligns independently to that
shared frame. This does not synchronize the camera shutters or perform stereo
calibration.

The [multi-camera AprilTag Python example](https://github.com/asadm/mighty-protocol/tree/main/examples/python/multi_camera_apriltag)
shows two camera previews with tag overlays, both VIO trajectories in one 3D
view, and relocalization notifications. It also supports recording and replaying
ROS1 bags. Follow the example's setup instructions to try it with two cameras.

## Configure from an application

You can also save tag settings from your own application. See
[SDK Recipes](/docs/sdk/recipes) for configuration examples. Stop and restart
VIO after changing settings through the SDK as well.
