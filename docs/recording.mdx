---
title: Recording
description: Record Mighty camera, IMU, barometer, pose, and calibration data for replay or offline analysis, and manage SD card bags from the visualizer.
---

Mighty can record ROS bag v1 files from the browser visualizer or directly to
its mounted SD card. Recording works independently of VIO — you can record in
Preview mode or concurrently while VIO is running.

## Record from the visualizer

All recording controls live in the **Recording** tab in the visualizer sidebar
(collapsed by default):

1. Plug Mighty Camera into your machine.
2. Open [mightycamera.com/viz](https://mightycamera.com/viz) and wait for the
   board to connect.
3. Expand the **Recording** tab in the sidebar.
4. Click **Start Recording** under **Browser Recording**.
5. Move the board through the motion you want to capture, then click
   **Stop Recording**.

The visualizer downloads a `.bag` file when recording stops. Keep the browser
tab open until the download starts.

In the same **Recording** tab, you can also toggle **Record to SD card
automatically with VIO** to start and stop an on-board SD card recording
whenever VIO starts or stops.

## Record on the board (button controls)

Mighty can write bags directly to its mounted SD card at `/mnt/sdcard` without
a browser attached. Button controls are orthogonal so VIO and recording can be
toggled independently:

| Button action | Result | Works in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Short press** (`< 1.2 s`, on release) | Toggle **VIO** (`Start VIO` / `Stop VIO`) | Preview or VIO (leaves recording untouched) |
| **Long press** (`≥ 1.2 s`, fires while held) | Toggle **SD Recording** (`Start` / `Stop`) | **Both Preview and VIO** (leaves VIO untouched) |

Board-side recordings are saved under `/mnt/sdcard` (`rec-NNNNNN.bag`) and
include:

- `/cam0/image_raw/compressed` — JPEG camera frames (`sensor_msgs/CompressedImage`)
- `/imu0` — accelerometer and gyroscope measurements (`sensor_msgs/Imu`)
- `/imu0/temperature` — IMU die temperature (`sensor_msgs/Temperature`, when available)
- `/barometer` — absolute atmospheric pressure in Pascals (`sensor_msgs/FluidPressure`, time-synced to Mighty's IMU clock when a MAVLink flight controller streaming `SCALED_PRESSURE` is connected)
- `/calib` and `/noise` — camera/IMU calibration and vibration filter metadata

## LED states (VIO + Recording)

The board LED shows **both** VIO health and recording status at the same time.
Recording never masks VIO tracking health or a VIO lost condition:

| VIO state | Not recording | Recording active |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Preview / Idle** | Slow single blip (`100 ms ON / 900 ms OFF`) | **Triple blip** (`120 ms ON/OFF ×3, 400 ms OFF`) |
| **VIO Tracking** | **Solid ON** | **Solid ON + double shutter wink** (`1200 ms ON`, brief double dark wink) |
| **VIO Starting / Recovering** | Fast even blink (`100 ms ON / 100 ms OFF`) | Fast **triple burst** (`100 ms ×3, 400 ms OFF`) |
| **VIO Degraded / Low Light** | Double blip (`50 ms ×2, 750 ms OFF`) | Double blip + **recording pulse** (`50 ms ×2, 250 ms ON`) |
| **VIO Lost** | Medium even blink (`200 ms ON / 200 ms OFF`) | **Medium even blink** (Lost always takes priority) |

If a long press flashes **4 rapid error blinks** (`60 ms`) and returns to the
previous pattern, recording could not start (most commonly because no SD card
is mounted at `/mnt/sdcard`).

## Browse, preview, repair, and download SD card bags

When Mighty is connected over HTTP/USB and in **Preview mode** (VIO stopped),
you can manage SD card recordings directly from the visualizer without removing
the SD card:

1. Stop VIO if it is running (the SD card browser is disabled while VIO is active).
2. Click **SD Bags** in **Controls / Pose** (or **Browse SD Card Bags** in the
   **Recording** tab).
3. Select any `.bag` file to view a **4-frame preview montage** sampled across
   the recording, along with duration, chunk count, and topics. Click any
   thumbnail to zoom in.
4. Use **Download Bag** to save the file over HTTP, or **Delete** (with
   confirmation) to free space on the SD card.

### Handling incomplete bags

If power is lost during recording, a bag may be left unindexed and marked
**Needs Repair** in the SD card browser. You can still inspect its preview
montage, then click **Repair Bag** (or **Repair & Download**) to rebuild its
ROS bag index in place on the device before opening it in standard ROS tools.
